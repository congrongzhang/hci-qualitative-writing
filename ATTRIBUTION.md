This package incorporates adapted workflow ideas from:

**Humanities Writing Companion** by `tizzy916`

Adapted concepts include selective reference loading, layered critique, author-voice and target-reader profiles, calibrated adversarial review, promise-delivery checking, traceable revision dossiers, writing-bottleneck support, and AI-use disclosure.

The material has been substantially transformed for qualitative HCI research. Changes include HCI contribution framing, CHI/CSCW venue routing, qualitative evidence states, Findings-to-Discussion transformation, theory-change testing, claim-evidence-contribution tracing, sociotechnical and ethics review, bilingual participant-quotation safeguards, and the user's anti-defensive writing requirements.

The original author has not endorsed this adaptation. The adapted package is shared for noncommercial use under CC BY-NC 4.0.
