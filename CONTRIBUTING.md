# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve HCI Qualitative Writing.

## Useful contributions

- Clarify an instruction that produces inconsistent writing support.
- Add a reusable checklist or worksheet for qualitative HCI work.
- Improve CHI, CSCW, or PACM routing while keeping venue rules marked for live verification.
- Report an evidence-boundary, privacy, attribution, or research-integrity concern.
- Add examples that use synthetic or fully anonymised material.

## Before opening a pull request

1. Keep the skill general enough to reuse across qualitative HCI projects.
2. Exclude manuscripts, participant data, confidential reviews, and identifiable research records.
3. Preserve the distinction among empirical evidence, researcher interpretation, theory, and recommendation.
4. Avoid fabricated citations, venue policies, quotations, or methodological details.
5. Update `ATTRIBUTION.md` when adapting a new external source.
6. Run `bash scripts/validate-package.sh` from the repository root.

## Pull request scope

Prefer one coherent change per pull request. Explain:

- the writing problem;
- the proposed behaviour;
- the files changed;
- a small example of the expected result;
- any attribution or licensing implications.

## Licensing

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under CC BY-NC 4.0. Confirm that you have the right to submit all included material.
