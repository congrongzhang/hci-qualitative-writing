## Intellectual fit

- [ ] The paper names the HCI or CSCW audience it advances.
- [ ] The primary contribution matches a form recognised by the venue.
- [ ] The contribution is visible in the abstract and introduction.
- [ ] The method and transparency standard match the chosen research paradigm.
- [ ] The paper explains why its context produces transferable knowledge.
- [ ] The subcommittee, area, or reviewer assignment choice fits the paper's central contribution.
- [ ] The manuscript length is proportionate to the contribution.

## Submission integrity

- [ ] All authorship and contributor decisions are confirmed.
- [ ] Related, prior, and parallel submissions are disclosed as required.
- [ ] Identifying information is removed where anonymisation applies.
- [ ] External links and supplementary materials preserve anonymity.
- [ ] Ethics, consent, privacy, and data-use statements are accurate.
- [ ] AI use is disclosed according to the current policy.
- [ ] The main paper stands independently where required.
- [ ] Files, metadata, figures, alt text, captions, and references pass final checks.
