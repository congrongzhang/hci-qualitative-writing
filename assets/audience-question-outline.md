## Signal check

- [ ] The title or heading identifies the purpose, question, or supported message.
- [ ] The opening gives the reader enough context to care about the specific problem.
- [ ] Every section answers a question that advances the main message.
- [ ] Every paragraph contains a point, supporting material, an interpretive step, and a link.
- [ ] Tables and figures state the question or message they support.
- [ ] Necessary definitions and method details appear where the reader needs them.
- [ ] Repeated background, unexplained jargon, and disconnected detail have been removed.
